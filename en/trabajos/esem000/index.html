The project

We run social media for ESEM, a Chilean clothing brand: reels, posts, stories and community on Instagram, plus its TikTok.

  • Reels
  • Instagram content
  • Community management
  • TikTok
01 The challenge

A clothing brand needs to show up every day, with a visual line people recognise and a community that responds, not only post when a garment drops.

02 The solution

We create the reel content, design the account’s posts and stories, and handle community management on Instagram. We also run its TikTok.

03 The result

We work in an orderly way: every step is logged in an online document the brand can review whenever it wants. This is ongoing work.
