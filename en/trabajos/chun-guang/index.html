Food · Restaurant · 2026

A new way to discover the menu

A neighbourhood restaurant's full menu turned into a digital experience you can browse in seconds from your phone.

Scroll for the menu
The challenge

Chun Guang had a long menu that only existed on paper and in photos passed around on WhatsApp. Anyone wanting to order ended up asking prices one by one, and the restaurant repeated the same answers every day.

The solution

We organised all 155 items into categories you can navigate without getting lost and put the food photography up front, because in hospitality the photo is what sells. We also rescued the restaurant’s existing visual identity and adapted it so it works digitally at the quality it deserved.

The result

The restaurant now has a single link that handles the whole conversation: customers see the menu with prices, choose, and go straight to ordering via WhatsApp or Uber Eats. The menu was built so the restaurant can change dishes and prices without rebuilding the site.

Nine categories, zero clutter

A 155-item menu stops being a problem when you browse it in parts. Each category opens only its own.
