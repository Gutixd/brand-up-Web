— Questions

Before you buy

Do I need to pay anything monthly?

No. It is a one-time payment. The stand is programmed with your link and works indefinitely, with no subscriptions or hidden costs.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. iPhones from the XS (2018) onwards read NFC natively by simply bringing the phone close, without opening any app. For older devices or phones with NFC off, the stand includes a QR code.

What if I do not have a Google listing yet?

We help you create and verify it before shipping the stand, at no extra cost. It is part of what we do as a marketing agency.

Can I change the link later?

Yes. If you move locations or want to point it to another profile, message us and we will walk you through reprogramming it.

Do you ship outside Santiago?

Yes, we ship across Chile. Shipping is included within the Metropolitan Region; other regions are quoted based on the address.
