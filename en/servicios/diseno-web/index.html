A good website is not a business card: it is an active acquisition tool. We build new sites, migrate existing ones and resolve technical issues like DNS and hosting so your digital presence works without interruption.

We design and build websites for businesses in Santiago and the wider Metropolitan Region: landing pages, corporate sites and service sites. Every site is custom designed, with no generic templates, and built to load fast and look right on mobile, where most visits come from.

We are based in Maipú and work with companies and entrepreneurs across Chile. Besides building new sites, we migrate existing ones and fix domain, DNS and hosting problems when a website stops working.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about web design & development

How much does a website cost in Santiago?

A landing page ranges from CLP $150,000 to $400,000 and a corporate site from CLP $350,000 to $900,000. The price depends on the design, the number of sections and the features you need. We always give a fixed price before starting.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page takes 1 to 2 weeks and a corporate site 3 to 6 weeks. The timeline depends mostly on how quickly we receive copy, photos and approvals.

Do you work with businesses in Maipú and other districts?

Yes. We are based in Maipú and work with businesses across Santiago, the Metropolitan Region and the rest of Chile. Work is coordinated over WhatsApp and video calls.

Can you fix or migrate an existing site?

Yes. We migrate sites to new hosting, correct DNS configuration and recover sites that are down. If rebuilding makes more sense than repairing, we tell you before charging.
