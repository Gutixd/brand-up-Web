In short

BrandUp creates social media content and manages Instagram, TikTok and Facebook accounts for businesses in Santiago and across Chile: reels, posts, stories and community replies. Monthly plans start from CLP $250,000. With businesses in Santiago and Maipú we can shoot on site.

We produce reels and social content with scripting, creative direction and professional editing, designed for reach and aligned with your brand identity.

We take care of your social media content: we shoot and edit reels, design posts and stories, and handle community management by replying to comments and messages. We work on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.

We have run social media for food businesses, clothing brands, educational institutions and health services. With businesses in Santiago and Maipú we can shoot on site; for the rest of Chile we work with the material they send us.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about reels & content

How much does social media management cost?

Monthly plans start from CLP $250,000. The final price depends on how many networks are managed and the amount of content per month.

Do you shoot the content?

Yes. We can shoot at your premises or work with material you already have. The arrangement is defined when quoting.

Which social networks do you work on?

Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. We recommend starting with the network where your customers already are.

Do the accounts stay in my name?

Yes. Social media accounts always belong to the client; we manage them with the access you give us.
