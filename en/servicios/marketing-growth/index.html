Frequently asked questions

Questions about marketing & growth

What is a digital marketing plan?

It is a document that defines which channels you will use, which actions come first and how they will be measured. It avoids spending on isolated actions that do not connect.

Is it useful for a small business?

Yes. A small business has a limited budget, which makes it even more important to know where to put it.

How much does it cost?

It is quoted case by case according to the size of the business and the channels reviewed. Monthly social media and advertising management starts from CLP $250,000, not including ad spend.

How soon will I see results?

It depends on the channel. Paid advertising shows data within days; ranking on Google takes weeks or months. We do not promise timelines that are outside our control.

Shall we talk about your project?

Tell us what you need and we will send a fixed-price proposal. We are based in Maipú and serve all of Santiago and Chile.
