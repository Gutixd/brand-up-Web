Branding & Identity

Your brand perception is worth more than you think.

A solid visual identity builds trust before the client reads a single line. We design brands that communicate professionalism, coherence and real differentiation within your industry.

We create brand identities for new businesses and for companies that need to tidy up or refresh their image: logo, colours, typography and the assets you will use every day on social media, your website and print.

The goal is for your brand to be recognisable and look equally professional everywhere it appears. We work with businesses in Santiago and across Chile, running the process remotely.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about branding & identity

What does a branding service include?

Logo and its versions, colour palette, typography and the applied assets agreed on, such as social media templates or stationery. The exact scope is defined in the proposal.

Do you do just the logo or the full identity?

Both. We can design the logo alone or develop the full identity with its applications.

Can you redesign an existing brand?

Yes. We review what you have, keep what works and update the rest so it looks consistent and current.

How much does branding cost?

It is quoted case by case, because it depends on whether you need just the logo or the full identity with applied assets. The quote is free and comes with a fixed price.

Shall we talk about your project?

Tell us what you need and we will send a fixed-price proposal. We are based in Maipú and serve all of Santiago and Chile.
