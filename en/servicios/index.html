FAQ

What we always get asked

How much does a website cost? +

It depends on scope: a landing page ranges from CLP $150,000 to $400,000, a corporate site from $350,000 to $900,000 and an e-commerce store from $600,000 to $2,500,000. You always get a fixed price before we start.

How long does a project take? +

A landing page takes 1–2 weeks; a corporate site, 3–6 weeks; an e-commerce store, 6–12 weeks. We give you a clear timeline at kickoff.

Do you include support and maintenance? +

Yes. Every project includes a post-launch support period, and we offer monthly maintenance plans: backups, security, updates and minor changes.

How many design revisions are included? +

We work with defined revision rounds (usually 2–3 per stage) to move fast without losing quality. Your feedback is part of the process, not an extra.

Do you work with clients outside Santiago? +

Yes, we work 100% remotely with clients across Chile and abroad. Meetings happen over video calls and day-to-day communication over WhatsApp.
