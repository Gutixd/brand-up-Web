Frequently asked questions

Questions about digital advertising

How much does digital advertising cost?

Monthly management starts from CLP $250,000. That does not include ad spend, which is paid separately and directly to Meta or Google.

Should I use Meta Ads or Google Ads?

It depends on how your customers look for you. Google Ads works when people already search for what you sell; Meta Ads works for getting known and showing your product. In the first conversation we recommend where to start.

Do you guarantee results?

No. No serious agency can guarantee sales or rankings. What we do is measure, show you the real data and adjust campaigns based on what works.

Do you run ads for local businesses in Maipú or Santiago?

Yes. Campaigns can be limited to one district or a radius around your premises, which is usually the most efficient option for a neighbourhood business.
